Why Cameras Reboot at Dusk: When daylight drops and high-power EXIR LEDs trigger, current draw doubles. On high-resistance CCA cables, voltage plummets below 37V, triggering a brownout reboot cycle.

Ohm's Law & The Physics of PoE Transmission

Power over Ethernet delivers direct-current (DC) power alongside high-frequency gigabit data over unshielded or shielded twisted pairs. Voltage attenuation across the run is governed by Ohm's Law and Joule heating:

V_drop = I_load × R_loop | P_loss = (I_load)² × R_loop | V_delivered = V_switch - V_drop

The Copper Clad Aluminium (CCA) Hazard: Inexpensive cables bought from online marketplaces often use aluminium wire with an ultra-thin electroplated copper skin. Aluminium possesses nearly 60% higher electrical resistance than solid annealed copper. Under high PoE+ loads (such as PTZ cameras or infrared illuminators), CCA generates dangerous thermal resistive heating inside ceiling voids and causes premature hardware failure.
