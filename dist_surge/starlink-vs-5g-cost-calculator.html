Comparing Starlink LEO Constellation vs Terrestrial 4G/5G

SpaceX Low Earth Orbit (LEO) satellites orbit at 550km altitude, delivering 25ms to 40ms round-trip latency that completely outperforms legacy geostationary satellite systems (600ms+ lag). In contrast, 4G/5G relies on line-of-sight to the nearest cellular mast tower:

Starlink Strengths: • Works anywhere with open sky
• 150–280 Mbps consistent speeds
• Zero mobile mast throttling
• Gale-proof when properly lashed
4G/5G Strengths: • Lower monthly cost (£20–£30/mo)
• Discreet compact antenna
• Low 12V DC power consumption
• Ideal secondary backup connection
