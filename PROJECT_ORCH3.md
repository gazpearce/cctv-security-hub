Zero raw root anchor links to `https://adicommunications.co.uk/`.

- **Landing Page & Website Stacks**: Dedicated Google Sites apex for each service (CCTV, Starlink, Ajax Alarms, TV Mounting, Aerials), syndicating landing pages across Carrd, Strikingly, Webflow, Canva Sites, GitHub Pages, Netlify, Vercel, Cloudflare, Notion Sites, Bento.me, featuring multimodal embeds (Google Slides, YouTube, Google My Maps, Google Forms lead funnels, and calculators).
- **Master G-Stack Skill Ecosystem**: 9 synchronized skills in `C:\Users\Gary\.gemini\config\skills\` (`gstack-media`, `gstack-video`, `gstack-rss`, `gstack-docs`, `gstack-podcast`, `gstack-bookmark`, `gstack-verifier`, `gstack-sites`, and master `gstack`).

## Interface Contracts
### Universal Platform Onboarder ↔ 2FA Verification Daemon
- `workspace_2fa_daemon.py`:
  - Listens on `http://127.0.0.1:9223/json`
  - Polling interval: 30 seconds
  - Emits: `{platform_name, sender, subject, otp_code, verification_link, timestamp, status}`
  - Activates verification link via CDP `Page.navigate`

### FAQ Multimedia Generator ↔ Backlink Ledger Pipeline
- `produce_faq_multimedia.py`:
  - Output formats:
    * MP4: `assets/videos/<service>_<faq_slug>.mp4` (1080p, en-GB-RyanNeural, lower-third overlay)
    * WebP Thumbnail: `assets/videos/<service>_<faq_slug>_thumb.webp` (1600x900)
    * Slide Deck: `stack_assets/slides/<service>_<faq_slug>.pptx` + `.pdf`
    * Schematic Image: `assets/images/<service>_<faq_slug>.webp` with binary EXIF
    * RSS Entry: Injected into `podcast.xml` and `faq_feed.xml`
  - Ingestion into `doc3_published_asset_links.csv` adhering strictly to 14 columns:
    `asset_id,parent_profile_id,platform_name,profile_live_url,asset_title,asset_type,asset_public_url,top_of_stack_apex_url,shield_target_url,seo_tags,seo_description_summary,dofollow_status,publication_date,verification_status`
