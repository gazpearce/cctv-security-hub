Transformer Volt-Amps & BS 7671 Compliance

Smart doorbells maintain continuous 2.4GHz/5GHz Wi-Fi transceivers, PIR motion sensors, and live video streaming buffers. Unlike simple momentary mechanical push-buttons, they draw continuous quiescent power:

Minimum Recommended Power = 24V AC @ 1.25A (30VA) | DIN-Rail Bell Transformer to BS EN 61558-2-8

When a visitor pushes the button, the doorbell momentarily shorts the power loop to fire the internal solenoid chime plunger while simultaneously transmitting video packets. Without a 20VA–30VA transformer, the sudden voltage dip triggers a processor restart.
