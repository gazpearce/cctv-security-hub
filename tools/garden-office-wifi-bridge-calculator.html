Fresnel Zone Trigonometry & Outbuilding Physics

Point-to-Point microwave radio does not travel in an infinitesimally thin laser beam; it expands into an elliptical football-shaped envelope known as the first Fresnel zone. The radius ($r$) at midpoint distance ($d$) for frequency ($f$ in GHz) is given by:

r_mid (metres) = 8.657 × √[ d_km / (4 × f_GHz) ] | 60% Clearance Required to Prevent Attenuation

60GHz Millimetre-Wave vs 5GHz: At 60GHz, the wavelength is only 5mm. The Fresnel zone is exceptionally narrow (< 0.5m), allowing millimeter-wave links to pass through tight gaps between eaves and fence tops with zero multi-path reflection or Wi-Fi channel congestion.
