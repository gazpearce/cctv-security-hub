Common TV aerial and coax faults

A TV reception problem may be caused by the television input, receiver, aerial, coaxial cable, connectors, wall outlet, splitter, amplifier, power supply, interference or local reception conditions. Comparing one working outlet with an affected one can help narrow down where to investigate.

Should I add a signal booster?

Not automatically. An amplifier can help in a suitable installation, but excessive amplification can overload a receiver and make reception worse. Signal strength and quality should be checked before adding or replacing amplification.

When is an engineer needed?

If reception keeps failing, several rooms are affected, outdoor cabling may be damaged, or an amplifier/distribution fault is suspected, an engineer can inspect the installation and measure signal levels and quality with appropriate equipment.
