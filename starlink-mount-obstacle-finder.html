How clear is your northern sky view?

Starlink satellites require a wide unobstructed cone to avoid dropouts during video calls/gaming.

Open Sky / No Obstructions

Clear view of northern sky. Standard short pole or direct wall mount is ideal.

Light Distant Trees or Low Roofline

Trees exist but are below or equal to roof level. Needs standard 1.5m extended mast.

Medium Obstructions / Nearby Foliage

Taller trees clipping satellite cone. Needs high elevation 2m - 3m roof mast.

Heavy / Dense Tall Tree Canopy

Surrounded by mature timber. Requires guy-wired super mast or off-roof tree/pole mount.

How to Host & Deploy This App for Customers & Mobile Phones

Step-by-step instructions for Gary Pearce to put this live on ADI Communications or use on mobile phones.

Method 1: Embed Directly on ADI Communications Website

You can embed this interactive tool directly into your official Starlink installation page (adicommunications.co.uk/starlink-internet-installation/):

  1. Save this file as starlink-tool.html on your web server.
  2. In WordPress or your site builder, add a **Custom HTML Block**.
  3. Paste the following code into the block:
<iframe src="/starlink-tool.html" width="100%" height="850px" style="border:none; border-radius:16px;" title="Starlink Solution Finder"></iframe>

Method 2: Host Free Online with Netlify or Vercel (Instant Mobile Link)

To give customers a quick short link or QR code on your van:

  1. Go to Netlify Drop (app.netlify.com/drop) or Vercel.
  2. Drag and drop the folder containing this index.html file.
  3. Netlify will instantly give you a free link like adi-starlink-finder.netlify.app.
  4. Share this link via SMS/WhatsApp with customers before you visit their site!

Method 3: Save to iPhone / Android Home Screen for On-Site Use

Gary Pearce can save this app directly onto his phone screen to calculate specs during customer site visits:

  • iPhone (Safari): Open the live link, tap the Share button, then tap "Add to Home Screen".
  • Android (Chrome): Open the link, tap the 3 dots menu top-right, then tap "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
