Understanding CCTV Storage & Compression Math

Surveillance storage calculations are governed by the relationship between raw sensor pixel resolution, compression efficiency, and dynamic motion duty cycles:

Daily Storage (GB) = [ (Camera Bitrate kbps × Compression Factor × Channels) / 8 ] × 86,400s × Duty Cycle / 1,000,000

Why H.265+ Outperforms Standard H.264: Traditional H.264 streams encode repetitive background data (such as stationary brick walls or empty driveways) in full I-frames at regular intervals. Hikvision and Dahua Smart H.265+ algorithms analyze surveillance scenes, generating long Group of Pictures (GOP) intervals and referencing static backgrounds. This reduces storage consumption by up to 70% without sacrificing 4K evidential facial clarity.
